Ten most frequently observed snowfall values

The most commonly observed snowfall value is 0 mm. It has 2008508
observations which shows the most recorded station-days had no snowfall.
The most frequent positive valaues include 25, 13, 51, and 76 mm. The
amounts are about 1,0.5,2, and 3 inches - respectively. This suggests
that measurements originally recorded in inches were converted and
rounded to mm. This is consistent with the US snowfall reporting
practices that measure new snowfall to the nearest tenth of an inch. \##
